## What OpenRive does

- **Real `.riv` files.** Reads and writes the Rive runtime format. Unmodified files round-trip byte for byte, which is
  checked against the runtime's 437 test files.
- **Official rendering.** The canvas is drawn by the official Rive WASM runtime, so what you see is what your app
  shows.
- **Design & animate.** Shapes, pen paths, text, images and SVG import, gradients, timelines with easing, state
  machines with inputs and pointer listeners.
- **Theme colors.** Define colors once and switch themes to recolor a whole file.
- **Automate.** A terminal UI, an MCP server for AI assistants, a REST API and in-editor scripts share one editing API.
- **Everywhere.** Web, self-hosted with Docker, or a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux.
- **Local and login-free.** Projects live in PostgreSQL: embedded on your machine, a server when you self-host.
